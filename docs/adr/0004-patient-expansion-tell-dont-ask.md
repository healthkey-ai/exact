# ADR 0004: Patient therapy expansion is the source's job — EXACT does not expand on the eligibility path ("tell, don't ask")

- **Status:** Proposed (codex + gstack /plan-eng-review folded in)
- **Date:** 2026-08-07
- **Deciders:** EXACT team + promop/CB owners (patient-data + vocab source of truth: Adam Blum).
- **Relates:** resolves the open question in **#263 (Phase-T)** and reverses its premise; narrows the eligibility-path role of the vocab mirror from [ADR 0002](0002-vocab-mirror-consumer-consistency.md) / [ADR 0003](0003-cb-exact-convergence-attestation-seam.md). Coordinates with promop's rich `PatientRecord` direction (promop #46/#54 healthkey-core).

## Context

EXACT matches a patient to trials on OMOP therapy concepts at three levels — regimen, component (drug ingredient), and type (drug class). Trial criteria are **pre-materialized** (`omop_therapy_*` columns, backfilled). The patient side arrives from the consumer (promop) as OMOP concept_ids.

Today (Phase P), when a patient's therapy arrives as a **regimen** concept (e.g. VRd) with its **components** already supplied by the consumer, EXACT matches directly. But when the consumer sends a regimen with **no components** (`patient_component_ids is None`, `trials/services/omop/therapy_graph.py:70-81`), EXACT does not expand it — component-level matching silently returns nothing (confirmed live in the #310 QA: patient 9009 arrived with `component_ids: []`; component-required trials were filtered out).

**#263 ("Phase-T")** proposed closing that gap by having **EXACT expand regimen→components locally**, traversing a release-pinned copy of the OMOP graph (`vocab_mirror.LocalConceptGraph`) **on the eligibility path**. That requires building the epic's hardest, still-unbuilt machinery: a retention-window release pin (the graph currently demands strictly-ACTIVE, conflicting with the per-request pin that may be a retained/superseded generation), a fail-closed `503 vocabulary_release_unavailable`, and a `MAX_MIRROR_AGE` staleness bound — because eligibility would then depend on the mirror being live and current.

## Decision

**Adopt "tell, don't ask": the patient-data source (promop) emits the already-expanded therapy components/types in its patient response; EXACT consumes them (Phase P, unchanged) and does NOT expand regimens on the eligibility path. Pure — no defensive fallback in the live matcher.**

Ownership:
- **promop** (patient-data + vocab source of truth): expand regimen→component/type in the patient response, with **provenance** (see Open Questions) and **release-pinning** (the concepts it emits are for a stated vocab release). This is the rich-`PatientRecord` contract (promop #46/#54).
- **EXACT**: keep consuming consumer-supplied components/types verbatim (no change to the match logic). **Enforce the contract, don't paper over it** — a record declaring a regimen with no normalized components must **degrade loudly / explicitly** (observable "unknown"), never a silent false-negative. The enforcement must be **consistent across the queryset prefilter AND the per-trial matcher**, and must distinguish **absent/unknown (`None`)** from **known-empty (`[]`)** — otherwise candidate selection and the rendered verdict can diverge. Today's quiet `(None, None)` return (therapy_graph.py:70-81) is the thing to make explicit.
- **Types too — extend the same principle, but stage it (sub-decision).** EXACT's OMOP-native **type** matching (`EXACT_OMOP_THERAPY_TYPES` / #286) currently validates patient *type* concept_ids **against the pinned mirror** (`type_release_gate.py`), and that validation **changes verdicts** — so the mirror is on a verdict path there even without graph traversal. End-state: "tell, don't ask" applies to types too — **promop emits release-valid type concept_ids; EXACT drops `type_release_gate` and does not re-validate** — which fully removes the mirror from the verdict path. **Recommended path (incremental, plan-eng-review):** do NOT drop the validator in the same step as the contract shift. **Keep `type_release_gate` transitionally as defense-in-depth** while promop ramps release-valid type emission and the release-skew check (below) lands; **retire it once promop reliably emits + the check is in place.** Consequence: while it stays, the mirror is a verdict dependency for types, so the enforce/fail-closed obligations below apply *to that path only* until retirement. "Make the change easy, then make the easy change" — don't couple removing the validator with the contract move.
- **The vocab mirror**: **retires from eligibility-path graph traversal** (the regimen→component expansion #263 proposed — confirmed there is no production traversal on the verdict path today — `LocalConceptGraph` is implemented-but-unwired (library + tests only, never called from the eligibility path)). Under the types sub-decision above it also retires from per-concept type validation. It keeps its still-valid roles: OMOP **title/presentation** resolution (trial-detail `omopConcepts`, exact#332) and the cross-artifact consistency gates. ADR 0002's "client-side whole-vocab consistency for **graph expansion**" rationale no longer applies to eligibility; the presentation-consistency rationale still does.

## Why (decision drivers)

- **Boundary integrity.** promop owns patient semantics + vocabulary interpretation; EXACT matches an already-normalized patient against already-projected trial criteria. Local expansion would make EXACT a second vocabulary-serving system with hard release/availability/staleness behavior.
- **It's the documented intent** — `CB docs/exact-downstream-and-omop.md`: "Patient side is the API consumer's responsibility (EXACT is stateless and does no expansion)."
- **Eliminates the epic's hardest forks** — retention-window pin, fail-closed 503, `MAX_MIRROR_AGE` all become unnecessary, because eligibility stops reading the mirror graph live.
- **Aligns with the convergence** — a rich, pre-expanded `PatientRecord` from promop is exactly the shared-projection direction (utah-rhtp brief §4.4; promop #46/#54).

## When local expansion (option A) IS legitimate — the escape hatch

Only for sources that genuinely cannot pre-expand: a direct external consumer, historical replay where source expansion is unavailable, or an explicit "raw OMOP concept only" API. Even then it is an **input-contract/normalization** concern — build it as a **separate normalization service** or a **time-boxed transitional compatibility mode with an end date**, NOT as live matcher behavior. A "thin defensive fallback" in the matcher is rejected: it becomes a shadow production dependency and reintroduces the very forks this ADR avoids.

## Consequences

- **#263 (Phase-T) is redirected**, not implemented: the retention-pin / 503 / `MAX_MIRROR_AGE` work is dropped from EXACT. Close/reframe #263 to point here.
- **New work lands in promop**: regimen→component/type expansion in the patient response, release-pinned + provenance-tagged (a promop feature — not eliminated work, moved to the right owner).
- **EXACT change (not trivial):** make the regimen-without-components path explicit/loud, and enforce it **consistently in the queryset prefilter AND the matcher**, distinguishing `None` (unknown → loud) from `[]` (known-empty), so selection and verdict agree.
- **Release-skew — correctness ≠ presentation.** Same-release compatibility matters for the things that gate a verdict: promop's **emitted concept_ids**, CB's **trial projection**, and any **mirror-backed validation** that remains (the type gate, while kept). Mirror **titles are presentation-only and must not gate a verdict** — a title skew is cosmetic, not a correctness bug. The existing attestation/release-match machinery (ADR 0002/0003) does **not** validate promop's *emitted* release. **Concrete checker (plan-eng-review):** promop stamps its patient response with the vocab release it expanded against; EXACT, at request entry (it already binds `active_release_id()` in `MatchingReleaseContext`), **compares the patient's stated release to the active mirror/projection release and fails closed + loud on mismatch** — never silently matches across a skew. That answers "who checks + how it fails" with a cheap request-boundary guard. Default to same-release across all three.
- **The projection-gate enforce** (`_PROJECTION_GATE_ENFORCE`, activation.py:161) was to flip **with Phase-T**. Redirecting Phase-T removes the graph-expansion trigger, but does **not** by itself mean eligibility never depends on the mirror: while the type gate (#286) stays enabled (the recommended transitional path), the mirror is still a verdict dependency and `_PROJECTION_GATE_ENFORCE` needs a trigger tied to *that*. Once `type_release_gate` retires (end-state), no mirror-backed verdict dependency remains and the enforce/fail-closed obligations fall away. **The types staging above therefore drives the enforce timeline.**
- **Repurpose the gating metric, don't delete it.** `record_regimen_unresolved` (phase_t_metrics.py) was the ADR's build-gating signal (how often a regimen arrives without components). Under this ADR it becomes a **contract-monitoring** signal: it measures how often promop **violates** the "always supply expanded components" contract. Keep it, wire an alert/dashboard — it is the health check for the new promop obligation.
- **`LocalConceptGraph` (traversal.py) is not deleted.** It is the implemented-but-unwired graph-traversal library (+ tests) #263 would have wired onto the eligibility path; keep it and mark it clearly **reserved for the escape-hatch normalization service (external / raw-OMOP consumer), NOT on the eligibility path.** Don't let it rot as silently-unused code — a one-line module note pointing here.

## Open questions (for review)

1. **Semantics of "components".** promop must define whether emitted components are exact regimen ingredients, temporally co-administered therapies, or inferred medications — and represent **provenance** so EXACT never treats inferred expansion as observed treatment. This is a promop contract decision.
2. **Release-skew policy.** "Same release" across promop-expansion / CB-projection / EXACT-mirror is safest; if cross-release is ever allowed, define the supported compatibility explicitly.
3. **EXACT enforcement shape.** Reject the input (4xx) vs degrade to a loud "unknown" per-axis. Leaning: degrade loudly + observable metric (never silent), reserve rejection for clearly-malformed input.
4. **Types staging.** Keep `type_release_gate` transitionally (recommended — enforce stays tied to it until promop is ready) vs drop it now with the component shift (bigger, couples two changes). Owner call; drives the `_PROJECTION_GATE_ENFORCE` timeline.
